Lab scripts
===========

Two Picos, one host. TX and RX on different CDC ports. Squelch open.
Payload list: test/corpus/air/ui.txt (one UI per line). Gap after TX is
2–3 s. bitFix sanity for these runs is none.

Live air, TX PWM noise, RX F1 then F0 (UI string in gtdump):

  test/run_pico_air_live_awgn.sh --tx /dev/ttyACM4 --rx /dev/ttyACM3
  test/run_pico_air_live_awgn_100.sh --tx /dev/ttyACM4 --rx /dev/ttyACM3

Default SNRs 3 3.5 4 5 6 7 8 (PWM-domain, not measured air dB).
Summary: test/logs/air_live_awgn/summary.txt

Dump each shot, then score the same WAV with Pico inject and atest:

  test/run_pico_tx_awgn.sh --tx /dev/ttyACM4 --rx /dev/ttyACM3
  test/run_pico_tx_awgn_100.sh --tx /dev/ttyACM4 --rx /dev/ttyACM3
  test/run_pico_tx_awgn_78.sh --tx /dev/ttyACM4 --rx /dev/ttyACM3

Host WAV (one Pico). gen_packets + Gaussian mix at a target in-band SNR,
then Pico inject F1/F0 and atest F1/F0:

  test/run_gen_awgn.sh --device /dev/ttyACM3
  test/run_gen_awgn.sh --gen-only --snr "3 4" --count 20

Default SNRs 3 3.25 3.5 3.75 4 5 6 7 8, 100 files each.
Corpus test/corpus/awgn_pn/<snr>/. measure.txt has requested and measured dB.
